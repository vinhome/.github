## ⚡ Mission

At **Vinhomes**, we build intelligent, resilient, and ultra-low-latency home automation systems. Inspired by aerospace-grade reliability, our hardware and software ecosystems seamlessly connect modern residences—transforming houses into self-optimizing living environments.

---

## 🛰️ Core Architecture

We focus on four foundational pillars to deliver a frictionless smart home experience:

* **Edge-First Intelligence:** Local AI processing for instant response times (`< 10ms`) and uncompromising user privacy—even when offline.
* **Unified IoT Mesh:** Native interoperability across Matter, Thread, Zigbee, and proprietary high-speed bus protocols.
* **Autonomous Energy & Climate:** Predictive HVAC, lighting, and power management driven by real-time occupancy and environmental telemetry.
* **Zero-Trust Security:** End-to-end hardware encryption, secure boot firmware, and continuous over-the-air (OTA) protection.
